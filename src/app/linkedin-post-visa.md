### LONG VERSION - Full Case Study

```
🎴 Case Study: How VISA at USF Created an AI Quiz to Promote Our Tournament

I'm proud to share a project I led for Vietnamese International Student Association at University of South Florida - an interactive personality quiz that's bringing attention to our Tiên Lên Tournament Season IV.

🎯 BACKGROUND:
Our club hosts an annual Tiên Lên (Vietnamese card game) tournament with an "Elite Council" theme. Past promotions relied on Instagram posts and word-of-mouth. We wanted something more engaging and memorable.

💭 MY VISION:
What if instead of telling people about the tournament, we let them EXPERIENCE the Elite Council? A personality quiz could:
• Immerse users in the tournament's narrative
• Create shareable content ("I'm Sangfroid! What are you?")
• Showcase our club's innovation
• Generate buzz before the event

🎨 DESIGN DECISIONS:

1. Conversational Experience
I designed it like a messaging app - bot messages on the left, user responses on the right. This feels more personal than a traditional form.

2. Personality System
Created 5 distinct Elite Council Bosses:
• Machination - Strategic mastermind
• Monomania - Intense obsessive
• Legilimency - Mind-reading perceptive
• Prescience - Future-seeing prophet
• Sangfroid - Emotionless calculator

Each needed unique dialogue, quotes, and personality traits that would resonate with different player types.

3. Timing & Animation
Questions don't flood the screen. Each appears with dramatic timing, letting users absorb the story. Suggestion buttons fade in after 600ms for theatrical effect.

4. Visual Identity
Dark royal aesthetic with:
• Custom color palette (Red Carriage, Ceramic Yellow, Mahogany)
• Space background + playing cards motif
• Glass-morphism effects for luxury feel
• Elegant Cinzel typography

👥 COLLABORATION:
Worked closely with a developer to translate my vision into reality. I handled:
• Concept & creative direction
• User experience design
• Personality writing & dialogue
• Visual branding
• Testing & feedback

⚙️ TECH USED:
Figma Make, React, Tailwind CSS, Motion animations

📊 RESULTS SO FAR:
✅ Completed in 2 weeks
✅ 100% responsive design
✅ 5 unique AI personalities
✅ Positive feedback from club members
✅ [Add engagement metrics if you have them]

🎓 LESSONS LEARNED:
• Collaboration between creative vision and technical execution is crucial
• Timing/pacing matters as much as visual design
• Interactive experiences > static promotional content
• Start with user journey, not features

Big shoutout to my development partner and the entire VISA at USF team for supporting this project!
```
